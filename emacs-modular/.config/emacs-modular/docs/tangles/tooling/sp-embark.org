* Embark

** embark

Bindings:

- =C-.= is =embark-act= (choose an action for the target).
- =M-.= is =embark-dwim= (run the default action).
- =C-h B= is =embark-bindings=.

Notes:

- =M-.= normally runs =xref-find-definitions=. This binding takes that over globally, and =embark-dwim= becomes the way to jump to definitions.
- The =:init= block sets =prefix-help-command=, so pressing =C-h= after a prefix key shows Embark's searchable list of the bindings under that prefix.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp -i
(use-package embark
  :ensure t
  :bind
  (("C-." . embark-act)
   ("M-." . embark-dwim)
   ("C-h B" . embark-bindings))

  :init
  (setq prefix-help-command #'embark-prefix-help-command))
#+end_src

** Embark keys under the leader

Keys:

- The map is =lem+embark-keys=, mounted on =sp-prefix= =E= (=C-; E=) with =bind-keys=, like the group maps in =sp-keys=.
- =a= is =embark-act=.
- =A= is =embark-act-all= (run one action on every candidate).
- =s= is =embark-select= (mark or unmark the candidate or target).
- =c= is =embark-collect= (keep the candidates in a buffer).
- =l= is =embark-live= (a collect buffer that follows the minibuffer).
- =e= is =embark-export= (open the candidates in a mode-specific buffer, e.g. =grep-mode=).
- =b= is =embark-become= (turn the current command into another one).
- =d= is =embark-dwim= (run the default action).
- =B= is =embark-bindings=.
- =C-,= in the minibuffer (=minibuffer-local-map=) is =embark-select=, for marking several candidates fast.

Why these keys:

- =E= is free under the leader (=e= is eval) and stands for Embark.
- The leader works in the minibuffer too, so the same keys serve =embark-act-all=, =embark-collect= and =embark-export= while a prompt is open.
- No =C-z=, =C-x=, =C-c=, =C-v=, no =C-SPC= and no =s-SPC=: those are clipboard keys, the input-source switch and Alfred.
- The commands are autoloaded, so the keys need no =:after embark=.
- =sp-prefix= is defined in =sp-keys=, which loads before this module.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp -i
(defvar sp-prefix)

;; Leader submenu: every Embark entry point under `sp-prefix' E.
;; The commands are autoloaded, so the keys work before Embark is loaded.
(bind-keys :prefix-map lem+embark-keys
           :prefix (concat sp-prefix " E")
           ("a" . embark-act)
           ("A" . embark-act-all)
           ("s" . embark-select)
           ("c" . embark-collect)
           ("l" . embark-live)
           ("e" . embark-export)
           ("b" . embark-become)
           ("d" . embark-dwim)
           ("B" . embark-bindings))

;; Quick toggle for marking candidates in the minibuffer.
(keymap-set minibuffer-local-map "C-," #'embark-select)
#+end_src

** embark-consult

Loads the glue between Embark and Consult once both are present, which adds actions such as exporting Consult results to a collect or occur buffer.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp -i
(use-package embark-consult
  :ensure t
  :after (embark consult))
#+end_src
