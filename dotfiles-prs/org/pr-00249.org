The tag chip colours in =sp-select-note= / =sp-insert-note= no longer use Notion's fixed hex pairs; they follow the active theme.

- =sp-org-tag-groups-palette= keeps its ten slot names (=default=, =gray=, ... =red=), so =sp-org-tag-groups-colors= is unchanged. Each slot now names a face (=error=, =success=, =link=, =link-visited=, =font-lock-keyword-face=, ...), a colour, or a mix =(A B WEIGHT)=; =nil= is neutral. The sp themes colour those faces from their palette, so any theme works through them.
- New =sp-org-tag-groups-palette-entry=: hue blended into the =default= background (0.22 light, 0.32 dark, as in #246), foreground nudged until contrast is at least 4.5.
- Recomputed on theme change via =sp-after-load-theme-hook=.
- A group with no colour, such as =unsorted= from #248, gets the neutral =default=. I did not touch the group list or colours, to avoid conflicts with #248.
- Doc (=.org=) and tangled =.el= edited together; CHANGELOG entry added; new =tests/sp-org-tag-groups-test.el= (5 tests, pass in Emacs 29 batch).

Not checked in a GUI: how the chips look in each theme. The face-to-slot mapping (e.g. =yellow= = =warning=, =pink= = =link-visited=) is my guess; adjust the palette if a colour looks off.
