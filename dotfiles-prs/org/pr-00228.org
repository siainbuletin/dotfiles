One doc, =docs/tangles/funct/sp-org-lint.org=, now tangles the whole =sp-org-lint= family into separate =.el= files:

- =sp-org-lint.el=: only =require=s for the submodules below.
- =sp-org-lint-others.el=: everything not in a bigger submodule (the list commands).
- =sp-org-lint-footnotes.el=: the footnote linter and its headless worker (the worker now loads only this file).
- =sp-author-notes.el=, =sp-author-notes-bibtex.el=, =sp-litnotes-refs.el=, =sp-litnotes-titles.el=: unchanged code, docs moved here from their own docs and from =sp-academic.org=.

The table of contents at the top of =sp-academic.org= lists the four author and litnote lint modules (marked "Author lint" / "Litnote lint") with links to the new sections and the commands named. README, CHANGELOG and =.el= docstrings point to the new place. =sp-split-leave-parents= is documented here as before but not required by =sp-org-lint=.
