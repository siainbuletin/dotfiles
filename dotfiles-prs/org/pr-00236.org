** Description
/Requested by *Sia* · [[https://claude.ai/code/project/chan_01KaQfLZaF43QUMN3kcT7pW2?thread=cmsg_01KaQfLZaF43QUMN3kcT7pW2HHkasK1mnHK76yFvmtt9Jo][project thread]]/

*Cause.* =org-table-widget= hides each row's source and draws the row as the =before-string= of an overlay. A click or =RET= is delivered to the hidden source, where =org-open-at-point= finds no link, climbs to the headline and offers every link of the entry. That is the menu.

*Fix* (in =sp-org-tables=, literate doc updated):

- A click follows the link under the pointer (advice on =org-open-at-mouse=, reading the =htmlize-link= property the widget keeps in its cells).
- =RET= (in =org-table-widget-map=) and =C-c C-o= on a row follow its link; a chooser appears only when the row has several links, since =RET= cannot know which was meant. A row without links keeps the old =RET= (=org-return=).

*Tested.* 5 ERT tests with a faked widget row, Emacs 29.3 batch (=tests/sp-org-tables-links-test.el=), and the tangle check. *Not run* with the real widget in a graphical Emacs (also the layout preserving =htmlize-link= across wrapped cells, which I read in the source but did not see run).
