** Description
/Requested by *Sia*/

*Before:* the lambda sat behind the left of the same area as the banner and boxes, the footer was centred under the lambda 15 lines above the bottom, and the lambda never changed size with the display.

*After:*

- *Two columns:* the lambda (=sp-dashboard-side-layer=) is the left column, centred in the window's height; the banner, title and boxes share the right column. The two are centred in the window together, =sp-dashboard-column-gap= apart. Offsets in =sp-dashboard-art-layers= now default to 0 and are relative to these places (offsets set in your config keep their number but not their old meaning).
- *Footer* centred in the window, two lines from the bottom (=sp-dashboard-footer-layer= is nil by default).
- *Lambda as SVG* (=sp-dashboard-side-as-svg=): each art line becomes =<text>= runs stretched to the character grid, and the image is cut into one slice per buffer line, so line heights are untouched. =sp-dashboard-side-scale= (=auto=, a number, or nil) sets the size; =auto= picks the largest factor within =sp-dashboard-side-scale-limits= at which the lambda fills =sp-dashboard-side-fill= of the window height and of the room left of the other column. A bigger window or display therefore gives a bigger lambda, while *all text stays at its normal size* (no font scaling any more).
- Without a graphical frame or SVG support, the plain text lambda is used. In a window too narrow for both columns the lambda is left out.
- Doc and CHANGELOG updated; the doc tangles to the =.el=.

*Checked:* Emacs 29.3 headless: banner ink, box corners and title share edges and centre at 200, 160 and 120 columns; footer centred on the last lines; =TAB=, privacy, Focus and clicking a TODO still work. In a real GUI Emacs under a virtual X display (DejaVu Sans Mono): the lambda renders complete, aligned to the grid, with no line gaps, at 190x58 and 240x70 frames (scale 1.15 and 1.38). *Not checked:* a Mac or 4K display, or other fonts.
