**** What changed
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-changed
:END:
- Cause of the lost help line: the batch questions (=a/g/s/q=, =d=, =u=) were =read-char-choice= prompts, so a long prompt replaced the echo-area hints of the report.
  - New =sp-batch-report-ask= shows the question as the same two-row help line (keys with their descriptions, plus a note line such as "Batch 1 of 3").
  - =n=, =p=, =TAB=, =S-TAB= and scrolling still work while it waits, so you can look through the batch before you answer (=sp-batch-report-ask-moves=).
  - Used by =sp-batch-report--ask-batch= (all =sp-batch-process=-style commands) and by every BibTeX lint batch question: author names, notes and surnames, duplicates, long keys.
  - =n= is no longer an answer: in steps 2 and 3 "normalise in the field" is =f=.
- Merging variants of one author (=sp-bibtex-lint-fix-merge=, step 4):
  - one group at a time, the report scrolls to it; =RET= takes the proposed name, =1=-=9= take that variant, =e= types a name.
  - =n= and =p= step to the next or previous group (as in the report), =c= clears a choice, =f= finishes, =q= or =C-g= stops.
- Step 1 of the author names (=Last, First=):
  - new =o= in the batch question: pick the entries to fix (comma separated, with completion); the others are not dropped, they come again in the next run.
  - new option =sp-bibtex-lint-pick-first= (default nil): ask which names to fix before every batch of that step.
