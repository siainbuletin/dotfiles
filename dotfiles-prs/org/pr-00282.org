Stacked on #281 (which is stacked on #280); retarget after those merge.

- *Matching:* =sp-author-notes--title-key= folds accents (=sp-author-notes-fold-diacritics=: ø ł ß æ œ đ, and everything that decomposes, so ă â î ș ț and the cedilla ş ţ), so =Søren=/=Soren Kierkegaard= find the same note, in =sp-author-notes=, =sp-author-notes-bibtex= and =sp-litnotes-meta=.
- *BibTeX escapes:* =S{\o}ren=, =Bulat{\u a}=, =\c{S}tefan=, =\"o=, =\'{\i}= ... are decoded to Unicode (=sp-author-notes-latex-to-unicode=), and the cedilla ş/ţ are written as the Romanian comma-below ș/ț. Applied to names and to =pub-title=.
- *File names:* new notes get an ASCII slug (=sp-org-slugify= folds accents); the title keeps the accents.
- *Duplicates:* several =person= notes that spell a name alike are named in the preview; apply asks which to use (=sp-author-notes-duplicate-function=, default: the spelling with the most accents). =M-x sp-author-notes-normalize-duplicates= asks per group which note to keep and, after one question, points the author and editor links of the litnotes at it (with backups). The other notes are listed, not merged or deleted.
