**** What I found
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-i-found
:END:
- =C-c o s= (=sp-sourcerefs-convert=) failed with =Invalid search bound (wrong side of point)=.
- Not the region or point handling in =sp-sourcerefs=: I ran it with and without a region, on the last line without a newline, with a reference list and with an existing Cited works block, all fine.
- The cause is in the BibTeX reader of =sp-cite-match=, which every command here calls first (=sp-cite-match-candidates=):
  - an entry was assumed to end at the next line that starts with =@=;
  - a field value can contain such a line (e.g. an abstract with =\n@handle=);
  - the value reader then moved point past that bound, and the next =re-search-forward= with the old bound signalled the error.
- Reproduced with a two-entry =.bib= whose abstract has a line =@lab for details=; the same bug would have hit =Ctrl+Shift+K= and =sp-cite-open= on such a library.

**** What changed
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-changed
:END:
- New =sp-cite-match--entry-end=: a brace entry ends at its matching closing brace. Fallback (parenthesis entries, unbalanced braces) is the old rule, the next =@= line.
- The field loop also stops when point is at or past the end, so it cannot search with a bound behind point.
- Docs: bullets in "Reading the BibTeX records" in =sp-cite-match.org=; =.el= re-tangled. =CHANGELOG.org= not edited.
