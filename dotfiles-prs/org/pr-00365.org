**** What
:PROPERTIES:

Some litnote =pub-title= lines enclose the title in quotes. =sp-litnotes-meta= now detects and fixes them.

- =sp-litnotes-meta--unquote= strips quotes (straight and curly) only when they wrap the whole value; inner quotes stay (="A" and "B"= is left alone).
- Batch fill (=-fill-all-dryrun= / =-convert=): a quoted title is its own category ("Quoted pub-title", counted and listed with old -> new). Convert removes the quotes with the existing backup, batch of 50, pick/choose and drop-and-stash handling. =sp-litnotes-meta-fill= (single note) does it too.
- The BibTeX title is read without enclosing quotes (={"Title"}=), so the changed-field update from #361 cannot reintroduce them.
- New =M-x sp-litnotes-meta-title-lint-dryrun= / =-convert= (on =sp-batch-report=, like the bib-parent lint) also reach notes with no citation key or no .bib entry.
- Literate doc updated (bullet-pointed); no CHANGELOG edits.
