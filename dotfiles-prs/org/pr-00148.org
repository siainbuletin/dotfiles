**** What
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what
:END:
Two new faces, =sp-text-face= (Org and other text modes) and =sp-minibuffer-face= (minibuffer and posframe), are added to =fontaine-faces=. Presets set =:sp-text-face-height= and =:sp-minibuffer-face-height=: 1.36 in the =t= fallback (what 4 =text-scale= steps of 1.08 came to) and 1.0 in =presentation=, whose =default= height is already 220.

- =sp-text-buffer= and =sp-minibuffer= now map those faces onto =default= with =face-remap-add-relative= instead of =text-scale-increase=. =sp-fonts-text-scale-steps= (#146) is removed. =text-scale= (~s-=~, =s--=) still works on top.
- =sp-posframe--set-text-size= still drops inherited remappings first, then maps =sp-minibuffer-face=.
- The plain minibuffer is now also at Org size (1.36); before it was two steps (about 1.17).
- Docs and CHANGELOG updated.
