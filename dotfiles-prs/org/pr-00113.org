** Description
/Requested by *Sia*/

*Before:* the boxes' lane was as wide as the EMACS banner (about 60 columns, so two boxes of 29), set by =sp-dashboard-lane-width= or the banner.

*After:*

- New option *=sp-dashboard-box-width=* (default 36): the width of each box, borders included. =M-x customize-variable= it, or =(setq sp-dashboard-box-width 44)=, to show more of each TODO.
- The lane is =sp-dashboard-agenda-columns= boxes side by side with a 2-column gap, whatever the banner's width; boxes shrink and drop to fewer columns in a window that is too narrow.
- The banner and the title are centred on the lane, and the right column of the dashboard (next to the lambda) is as wide as the lane or the banner, whichever is wider, so the lambda's scale and the two-column layout follow the new width.
- =sp-dashboard-lane-width= is removed (it is replaced by the option above); nothing else referenced it.
- Doc and CHANGELOG updated, the doc tangles to the =.el=; I also corrected two sentences that still said title widths were measured in pixels.

*Checked* (Emacs 29.3): headless extents at 200, 160 and 120 columns (boxes 74 wide, banner and title centred on them, footer centred); TAB, privacy and Focus tests unchanged; graphical Emacs on a virtual display shows the wider boxes with the banner centred over them. *Not checked:* your Mac, 4K display and your own box-width choices.
