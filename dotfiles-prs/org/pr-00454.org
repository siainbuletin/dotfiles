**** What it does
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-it-does
:END:
- New module =sp-fetch-inbox= (doc: =docs/tangles/academic/sp-fetch-inbox.org=), =M-x sp-fetch-inbox= or =C-c w b a=.
- Items: records of the =inbox= group in DEVONthink =literature=, plus PDFs in =~/Documents/literature/inbox/= (=mac-bibdesk/= skipped).
- Asks for the =.bib= file, reads the DOI from the first two PDF pages (=pdftotext=), fetches the entry with org-ref, generates the key with the =bibtex-autokey= scheme (letter suffix if taken).
- Shows the PDF left and an editable BibTeX buffer right. =C-c C-c= files, =C-c C-k= aborts, =C-c C-r= new key, =C-c C-d= fetch another DOI.
- After one yes/no question: PDF moved to =~/Documents/literature/KEY.pdf=, DEVONthink record renamed =KEY.pdf= and moved to the literature group, =file= field added, entry appended to the =.bib= and saved. Nothing changes before the question.
- Embark: =f= on a DEVONthink record files it.
