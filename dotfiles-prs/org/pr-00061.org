**** Summary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: summary
:END:
Removes the conditional-loading guards from the module =.el= files and their docs:

- =sp-load-extras= is removed from every =when=/=:when=/=:if= condition, and the =defcustom= and the =--no-extras= command-line switch are removed from =early-init.el=.
- The =(locate-library "x")= checks in =use-package= =:when=/=:if= keys are removed as well. Other conditions stay: =ui/sp-faces= keeps =:when (image-type-available-p 'svg)= and =ui/sp-frame= keeps =:when (eq system-type 'darwin)=.
- =sp-evil= no longer wraps its forms in =(when sp-load-extras …)=: the body is unwrapped and dedented, and its commentary is updated.
- Commented-out guards and the doc prose that described them ("when extras are enabled and the package is installed") are adjusted.

Left in place: two =(when (locate-library "nerd-icons") (require 'nerd-icons))= forms (=sp-splash=, =lem-dashboard=). They guard a =require=, not a package declaration; say if they should go too.
