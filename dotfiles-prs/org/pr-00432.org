***** Fit in a half frame (macOS, AeroSpace)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: fit-in-a-half-frame-macos-aerospace
:END:
- The widths are worked out from the display, not fixed: =sp-citations--half-frame-columns= = (display width - 24 AeroSpace gaps) / 2, minus the frame's internal border (30 on each side), times =sp-posframe-width-fraction= (0.75), divided by =frame-char-width= of the =default= face (150, Aporetic Sans Mono).
- Order is kept: emoji, authors, year, title, citekey, keywords.
- 27 columns are fixed (margin, Vertico mark, indicators, type emoji, year, separators). The rest: authors take a fifth (10 to 30), the citekey takes up to 45 but never leaves the title under 20 (nor itself under 16), the title takes what is left, tags only come back at 110 columns or more.
- *Trade-off:* the citekey is only whole (45) from about 110 columns in all. With a font this size that is a display of roughly 1900 points or more for a half frame. Below that the key is cut first: 80 columns give a key of 22, 90 give 30, 104 give 42. At the base size on a laptop (about 68 to 80 columns) a 45-character key does not fit next to a readable title. Levers: =sp-posframe-width-fraction= (0.75 to 0.9 gives +20%), a smaller font, or =sp-citations-layout-columns= to set the columns by hand.
- =M-x sp-citations-fit-layout= works the widths out again (after a font or frame change) and refreshes.

***** Exact emoji cells
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: exact-emoji-cells
:END:
- *Cause found in the code (not seen on screen):* =citar= pads and cuts by =string-width=, but an emoji is drawn by the emoji font, with its own advance width. A variation selector (U+FE0F, in 🌿️ 📁️ 📝️ 🎓️) or a ZWJ sequence (the master's thesis emoji) changes what is drawn but not the character count. Every row with a different emoji ended at a different pixel. The earlier attempts (#406, #409, #427) used =:align-to= columns from the window's left edge, which also depend on the Vertico mark, and never measured the glyphs.
- *Fix:* each emoji (indicators and entry type) goes in a cell measured with =string-pixel-width= in the font in use: the cell is N spaces (so =string-width= is exact and =citar= never pads or cuts it) with a =display= property of the emoji plus a relative space of the pixels left up to N character cells. An emoji wider than its cell is made smaller (face =:height=). Absent indicators are a pixel-exact blank. No =:align-to=, so the line start does not matter.
- Cached by emoji, columns, character width and font. On a terminal it falls back to the emoji plus spaces.
- *Not verified:* I have no graphical Emacs here, so I could not measure the real emoji widths (Apple Color Emoji). The batch tests use a stubbed =string-pixel-width=. The check is by eye (see below).
