Stacked on #45 (merge #45 first and GitHub retargets this to =main=).

Before: the menu was a flat list (Agenda, Config, Mail, Notes, Projects) and =wawawewa= was centred on the window.

After:

- The menu is =sp-splash-menu=, a list of blocks, each drawn in a box of lines (=sp-splash-box-face=, inherits =shadow=) centred under the banner. Agenda is a block with nested rows: *General agenda* (=a=), *Thesis* (=t=), *Issues* (=i=), *Deadlines* (=d=). Config (=c=) and Notes (=n=) are single-row boxes.
- Each agenda has a variable for its files: =sp-splash-agenda-general-files=, =-thesis-files=, =-issues-files=, =-deadlines-files=. While one is nil it uses =org-agenda-files=; General with nil keeps opening the agenda workspace. Deadlines shows only deadlines in the next =sp-splash-deadlines-days= (30).
- Mail and Projects (rows, keys =m=/=p= and their commands) are removed.
- =wawawewa= is italic and centred on the lambda (=sp-splash-footer-layer=), so it sits near the art.
- =sp-splash.org= updated in step (new "Splash menu" section, agendas section); it tangles to the same text as the =.el=. CHANGELOG entry added.

Not in this PR: the sidebar for writing projects and the issue tracker (own thread), and the theme colour framework (next PR).

Checked in headless Emacs 29.3 (200x50, =nerd-icons-faicon= stubbed): three boxes under the banner with the nested agenda, footer under the lambda, and the deadlines command listed a test deadline 3 days ahead. Not checked: a real GUI frame with nerd icons and your fonts (box characters need a font with box drawing).
