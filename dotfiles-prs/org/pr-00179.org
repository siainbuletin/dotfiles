**** Is it fontaine?
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: is-it-fontaine
:END:
Probably not (read from the sources of fontaine, posframe and vertico-posframe; not run). =fontaine= only sets faces and knows nothing about child frames. =posframe= sizes and positions the child frame with the parent's =default-font-width= and =default-line-height=, so a larger font in the child needs more room than it gets. The doc lists what is not ruled out (font-spec vs string, points vs pixels) and three next steps.
