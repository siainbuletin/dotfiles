** Description
/Requested by *Sia*/

*Before:* the banner and the boxes were not on the same vertical lane. Cause: the column the title and boxes centre on was worked out /before/ the window margins were set, which narrow the window, while the banner is placed using the narrower width afterwards. In my headless runs that left the banner about 8 columns left of the boxes. The agenda grid (86 wide) was also wider than the banner (60), and the Config/Notes boxes were narrower still.

*After:*

- The column is worked out again once the margins are set.
- The agenda boxes and the Config/Notes boxes are laid out together in one lane, as wide as the banner and centred on it, so the outer edges of the boxes line up with the banner and the title. The lane is split into =sp-dashboard-agenda-columns= equal columns (2 by default), so the boxes still grow sideways and down.
- New =sp-dashboard-lane-width= (nil = banner width). =sp-dashboard-agenda-box-width= is removed (the width now comes from the lane). =sp-dashboard--menu-rows= / =--insert-menu= are replaced by =--menu-box= / =--insert-boxes=.
- Doc and CHANGELOG updated; the doc tangles to the =.el=.

*Checked* (Emacs 29.3, headless, frames of 200, 160 and 120 columns): banner ink, box corners and title have the same left/right edges and centre; =TAB=, privacy mode, Focus and clicking a TODO still work. *Not checked:* a GUI. With the lane at 60 columns each box has about 25 columns of text, so TODOs wrap more; raise =sp-dashboard-lane-width= to widen them (the banner stays put).
