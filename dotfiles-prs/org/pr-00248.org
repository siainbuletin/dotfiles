Settles ADR 0020 and implements it.

- *Unsorted group:* a tag in no group is allowed. =sp-org-tag-group-of= answers =("unsorted")=: no emoji, default colour, no warning. The tag menu heads them =unsorted=, last. =M-x sp-org-tag-groups-list-unsorted= lists them.
- *Registry from groups:* =sp-org-tag-registry.el= now sets =sp-org-tag-groups-alist= and =sp-org-tag-groups-ungrouped= and derives =org-tag-persistent-alist= from them (=sp-org-tag-groups-to-alist=, Org's group-tag form). =sp-org-tag-add= asks for the group and inserts the tag in that element. =sp-org-tag-groups= no longer holds a copy of the data. Group names are registered tags and are in their own group.
- *ADR 0020:* records both decisions and what was implemented, including that a member named like its group is left out of the Org form.
- Docs (=.org=) and tangled =.el= edited together; tests and CHANGELOG updated.
