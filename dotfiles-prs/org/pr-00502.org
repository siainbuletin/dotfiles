**** What changed
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-changed
:END:
- Table columns have a fixed width: as wide as the widest cell, at most =sp-batch-report-column-max-width= (40).
  - The rest of a cell wraps inside its column, on the lines under it: at spaces, a long word is cut; links and faces stay.
- When the columns do not fit the window, the widest are narrowed first, down to =sp-batch-report-column-min-width= (8), so no row runs off the window.
- The width is counted in characters of the buffer's own text size, so zooming the text counts too.
- The tables are laid out again by themselves, with nothing to press:
  - triggers: =window-size-change-functions= (frame resize, split), =window-configuration-change-hook=, =text-scale-mode-hook=;
  - it waits for 0.2 s idle, so dragging a frame edge draws once; nothing happens when the width is unchanged or the report has no table.
- Drawing again keeps the folded sections, the dropped entries, the point (same row, same offset) and the top line of each window.
- A row is still one entry for =n=/=p=, =d= and =u=.
