Before: once =pulse= was loaded, pressing =C-x o= failed with =apply: Wrong number of arguments: #<subr other-window>, 0=.

After: =C-x o= switches windows normally. The line still pulses on window change, and through =pulsar=.

=sp-faces= advised =other-window=, the scroll commands and =recenter-top-bottom= with =pulse-line= using =:after=. Because =pulse-line= is interactive (it's bound to =C-<return>=), nadvice used its empty =(interactive)= spec in place of the advised command's own spec, so =other-window= got no COUNT.

How: this drops the =advice-add= loop from =libraries/modules/ui/sp-faces.el= and from its literate doc =docs/tangles/sp-faces.org=. The doc's prose now says why the advice was removed. Those commands already pulse through =pulsar-pulse-functions= in =sp-windows=. The =C-<return>= binding and the =window-selection-change-functions= hook stay as they are.
