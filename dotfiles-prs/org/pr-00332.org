**** What
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what
:END:
- =sp-litnotes-meta-fill= (and the =citar-create-note= advice) also handles three lines:
  - =read-status=: set to =02-preparing= without asking.
  - =relevance-factor= and =read-priority=: prompted, only =01= to =05= accepted, =01= is the most relevant / read first. Empty answer skips.
- The =base-text= schema (in =sp-vulpea.org=, tangled to =sp-vulpea-schemas.el=) requires all three, as strings with =:one-of=:
  - =read-status=: =01-current=, =02-preparing=, =03-review=, =04-paused=, =05-done=.
  - =relevance-factor=, =read-priority=: =01= to =05=.
- =M-x sp-litnotes-meta-status-report=: lists every litnote (status, priority, relevance, title) with counts per status in the header line; statuses outside the list show as a warning.
  - =m=/=u= mark, =M= marks all of one status, =U= clears, =s= changes the status of the marked notes (or the note at point), =g= rescans, =RET= opens.
- =M-x sp-litnotes-meta-backfill-children=: in a book or collection litnote, finds BibTeX entries whose =crossref= is its key and that have no note, lets you pick them (empty = all), and makes each through =citar-create-note= (so =bib-parent= links this note).
  - =bib-project= is copied from the parent; =relevance-factor= and =read-priority= are asked once for the batch.
- The draft query in =sp-vulpea-org-ql= now treats =01=/=02= as relevant (it used ~>= 4~ before the scale was flipped).
