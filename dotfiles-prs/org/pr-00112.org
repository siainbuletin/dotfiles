/Requested by *Sia* · [[https://claude.ai/code/project/chan_01KaQfLZaF43QUMN3kcT7pW2?thread=cmsg_01KaQfLZaF43QUMN3kcT7pW2T63Kp9dkm1NruXo3BFatDY][project thread]]/

*Problem:* Dirvish listed two sets of icons per entry.

*Cause:* =sp-dired= gives Dirvish its own icons (=nerd-icons= in =dirvish-attributes=). =sp-icons= also turns on =nerd-icons-dired-mode= from =dired-mode-hook=. Dirvish buffers are derived from dired, so that hook ran in them too and both drew an icon.

*Change:* =sp-icons--dired-off-in-dirvish= turns =nerd-icons-dired-mode= off from =dirvish-mode-hook= and =dirvish-directory-view-mode-hook= (parent and preview windows). Plain dired keeps its icons. Module doc and CHANGELOG updated.

*Not checked:* nothing was run (no Emacs with Dirvish and nerd-icons in the container). If =dirvish-mode= turns out to be enabled before =dired-mode-hook= runs, the order matters and this needs a look.
