**** What changes
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-changes
:END:
- Both keys act only while DEVONthink is in front (the hotkeys are switched on and off by an app watcher).
- =Cmd+Z=: runs Edit > Undo only when its title says highlight, annotation or markup (e.g. "Undo Add Highlight"). Otherwise the normal Cmd+Z goes to DEVONthink.
- No stale highlights: the Edit menu always shows the next undo step. A highlight that is gone, or that came before another action, is not touched.
- =Cmd+Shift+D=: runs View > "Hide Inspector" or "Hide Sidebar" when one is shown. Otherwise the normal Cmd+Shift+D goes to DEVONthink.
- =DEVONthink.dumpMenu("Edit")= in the Hammerspoon console prints a menu's titles, to fix the patterns if needed.

**** Things to check

- If DEVONthink does not put highlights on the undo stack, Cmd+Z will pass through unchanged and nothing is deleted. Tell me the Edit menu text after making a highlight.
