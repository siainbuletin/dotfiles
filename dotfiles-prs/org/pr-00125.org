** Description
*Before:* a flavor needed twelve hand-picked colours, and the engine only knew =dark= and =light= as flavor names.

*After:*

- =sp-theme-flavor-from-palette COLORS &key variant roles= turns a handful of =#rrggbb= colours into a flavor:
  - the darkest colour (pushed darker) is the background of a dark theme and the lightest (pushed lighter, contrast >= 10) the foreground; the other way round for a light theme;
  - the two most saturated remaining colours are =accent-1= (also the cursor) and =accent-2=, lifted to contrast >= 4.5;
  - the six status hues (red ... cyan) stay recognisable but are not new colours: the usual hue blended 30% towards =accent-1=;
  - =:roles= gives a colour to a job, e.g. =((red . 4) (blue . "#6fa8ff"))= (an index into the palette or a hex).
- =sp-theme-import-palette NAME COLORS= (interactive; accepts a pasted string of colours) registers the flavor, writes =libraries/themes/sp/sp-NAME-theme.el= (three forms, the palette stays in the file) and loads it. Flavors can now carry a =:mode= (=dark=/=light=), so their names are free.
- Example: =sp-wandavision=, from the five colours of [[https://www.color-hex.com/color-palette/111848]] (=#2a3156 #8f8280 #4e5044 #8a4160 #8d2025=), dark: background =#171b2f=, foreground =#cdc7c6=, accents =#b77375= and =#ae7a90=.
- The registry reads the background of such theme files.
- Also: the registry tests expected the Modus themes and a count of 70+, which no longer matches =main= (they failed on =main=); they now match the remaining themes.
- 4 new ERT tests; engine 13 and themes 13 pass on Emacs 29.3.

*Not checked:* how =sp-wandavision= looks in a graphic frame (only that it loads, its colours, and the contrast ratios). The palette is dark and muted, so the accents are lifted a lot to stay readable; try =M-x sp-load-theme= and =sp-theme-set-color= / =:roles= to adjust. It is not in any family yet.
