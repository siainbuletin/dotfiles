**** Summary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: summary
:END:
=M-x sp-author-notes-bibtex= made Emacs unresponsive. It was not a missing file: every author note was compared with every author of every entry (people × entries × authors, each comparison allocating strings). With #229's slices it still froze, because one author note is one indivisible step and took seconds on a big =.bib=.

- The entries without a litnote are indexed once (new "indexing" stage) by full author name and by last name plus first initial; a note looks up only its own keys. Results and their order are unchanged.
- Missing files: a file of =sp-bibtex-files= that cannot be read is skipped with a message. No files, or none readable, stops with a =user-error= naming them and suggesting =sp-bibtex-register=.
- Doc (=sp-org-lint.org=) tangled, CHANGELOG entry added.

**** Measurements (synthetic vault, Emacs 29.3)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: measurements-synthetic-vault-emacs-293
:END:
- 3,000 author notes, 500 entries: 48.7 s before.
- 3,000 author notes, 40,000 entries (31 MB =.bib=), 5,000 litnotes: about 7 s after (3.8 s reading the =.bib=), in slices. Before, extrapolated, hours.
