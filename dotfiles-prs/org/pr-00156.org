**** What
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what
:END:
The =M-x= text still glitched in Org after #152 and #154, and the cause was not found. As asked, =sp-posframe= is rewritten as a plain setup: =vertico-posframe= centred, 4 pixels of padding (border in the background colour), terminal fallback. That is the whole module.

Removed:

- the nano-style prompt and the dotted line (=sp-posframe-divider-*=, =sp-posframe-nano-commands=),
- the dimming (=sp-posframe-dim-*=),
- the fixed width (=sp-posframe-width-fraction=) and the text size reset,
- =sp-posframe-debug-minibuffer=.

Also removed: =sp-minibuffer= is no longer on =minibuffer-mode-hook= in =sp-fonts=, so the minibuffer text is at the =default= size (the function and =sp-minibuffer-face= stay). A larger font than the frame was sized for is a common cause of clipped text in a posframe. This also changes the size of the plain minibuffer.

Docs: =sp-posframe.org= rewritten (with a section on what was removed), plus =dividers-and-lines.org=, =sp-fonts.org=, =main.org= and =CHANGELOG.org=.
