A design record only (=adr/0020-tag-families.org=, status /proposed/, plus its row in =adr/README.org= and a CHANGELOG line). No code changes.

*Decision proposed:* families of note tags are one alist, =sp-org-tag-families=, of =(FAMILY TAG…)= lists, which is the shape of Org's own =org-tag-groups-alist=. =org-tag-persistent-alist= (#105) is derived from it as Org group tags (=:startgrouptag= ... =:endgrouptag=), so a tag search on a family finds its members. The record also lists the alternatives (raw group-tag syntax, plists, structs, nested names, vulpea properties, symbol plists), the consequences for =sp-org-tag-lint=, =sp-tag-emoji= and the prompts, and two open questions (family names, whether a tag in no family is refused).

*Checked* (Emacs 29.3, headless): the family list converted to the group-tag form and =org-tag-alist-to-groups= returned the same families (a =("group" . ?g)= member kept its key); in an Org buffer a =+music= match found a heading tagged =album=. *Not checked:* the converter and lookup functions (sketched in the record only), nested families, org-ql / vulpea behaviour with group tags, the completion grouping.
