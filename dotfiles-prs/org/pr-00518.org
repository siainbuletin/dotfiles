**** What changed
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-changed
:END:
- *Organisations as authors* (=The editors=, =Mute Collective=):
  - The =Last, First= rewrite no longer touches names that look like an organisation (=sp-bibtex-organisation-patterns=: =The ...=, Collective, Committee, Association, University, Institute, Press, Group, editors, ...). They are flagged instead.
  - New step 0b of =sp-bibtex-lint-fix-authors= (also =M-x sp-bibtex-lint-fix-organisations=): per batch, =o= = organisations (written as ={{Mute Collective}}=), =P= = persons (converted in step 1), =e= = each name, =s=, =d=, =u=, =q=; =n=/=p= still browse the report.
  - In the author and lint reports, =o= and =P= mark a name of the entry at point (any name, also one the patterns miss); the report redraws.
  - Answers are remembered in =sp-bibtex-organisations-file= (=:organisations=, =:persons=), so they are not asked again. A remembered organisation is wrapped automatically, and a name already damaged to =Collective, Mute= is repaired.
  - Names in braces are skipped by the typo, same-surname, prefix and merge steps.
- *BibTeX accents* (=C{\'e}sar Bola{\~n}o=):
  - Matching the author notes decodes the accents, so it finds the note =César Bolaño=. The field keeps its escapes and the braces survive (=Bola{\~n}o, C{\'e}sar=).
  - =sp-bibtex-names-unicode= (off by default) writes =Bolaño, César= instead.
  - Aliases added to a note are written in Unicode.
  - The space of an unbraced accent (=\c c=) no longer splits a name.
- No note is created by the lint (unchanged). =CHANGELOG.org= not edited. Docs updated as bullet lists in =sp-academic.org=, tangled to =sp-bibprocess.el=.
