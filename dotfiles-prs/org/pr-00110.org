**** What changes
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-changes
:END:
- =sp-org-tag-families=: the families of note tags as a list of =(FAMILY TAG…)=, in the shape ADR 0020 proposes, with the six families from the ADR (structure, who, music, software, literature, inquiry). =sp-org-tag-family-of= returns a tag's families.
- =sp-tag-emoji-family-colors= gives each family one of Notion's ten tag colours; =sp-tag-emoji-palette= holds the pairs, a tinted background with dark text on a light theme, a muted background with light text on a dark theme (read from the frame's =background-mode= every time the selector draws).
- In =sp-select-note= and =sp-insert-note= the tags in the right-hand column are now chips: the name with a space on each side on the family's background. A tag in no family, or a family with no colour, gets Notion's default grey. Chips that do not fit the column become an ellipsis. Tags are still matchable (=deep=, no =#= now).

**** Not done
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: not-done
:END:
=sp-org-tag-registry.el= does not use =sp-org-tag-families= yet: =org-tag-persistent-alist= is still the flat list, so the two are kept in step by hand until ADR 0020 is decided. Nested families are not followed. This PR is not blocked on the ADR, but it takes its shape for granted.
