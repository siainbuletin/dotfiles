**** Summary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: summary
:END:
- *Request:* keep the hints in the echo area for good, or let a key show them again.
- *Not done: nano's =sit-for= loop.* =(sit-for 30)= blocks the command until a key is pressed or the time passes; it would freeze the BibTeX buffer's commands. Two non-blocking things do the same job instead:
  - =C-c ?= (=sp-echo-help-show-now=) in any buffer with =sp-echo-help-mode= shows the hints at once, replacing the current message. Without hints it says so.
  - An idle timer (=sp-echo-help-idle-delay=, default 1.5 s, nil turns it off) shows the hints again when the echo area is empty. One shared timer; it stops when no buffer uses the mode.
  - Together with the existing re-show after each command, the hints stay there unless a message of a command is showing; that message is never overwritten, and the hints return on idle.
- Docs (bullet lists): new part "Keeping the hints visible" in =sp-interaction.org=; =.el= re-tangled. Two byte-compile warnings of #457 fixed (long docstring line, free variable).
- Tests (Emacs 29.3 batch): 2 new (show-now, timer start/stop), 6 of 6 pass in =sp-echo-help-test.el=; 15 of 15 in =sp-fetch-inbox-test.el=. No CHANGELOG edit.
- *Untested by Claude:* the real GUI, whether =C-c ?= is free in your other modes (it is not used in the config; =C-c= stays in CUA's sense of a prefix only without a region), and eldoc or Embark clearing the echo area.
