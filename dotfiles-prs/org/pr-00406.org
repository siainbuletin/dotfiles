**** Summary
:PROPERTIES:

:END:
- Candidate width now follows the posframe (=sp-posframe-width-fraction= of the frame) instead of the whole frame; =sp-citations-candidate-margin= keeps 2 columns free.
- All template columns are =*= columns. Each column is fitted to the longest value among all entries (so rows stay aligned): narrow columns (citekey, year, type) keep their full width, and the title, authors and tags share the rest.
- New year column after the authors; a column empty for every entry (e.g. =beref=) is dropped with its spacing.
- Cut text stays hidden in the candidate, so matching still sees the full title and authors.
- Implemented as =:around= advice on =citar--format-candidates= (=sp-citations--format-candidates=); templates without a =*= column fall back to citar's own function.
- Docs updated in =docs/tangles/academic/sp-academic.org= (bullet lists); =sp-citations.el= is the matching tangle. No CHANGELOG edit.
