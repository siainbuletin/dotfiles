**** What
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what
:END:
New module =sp-see-also= (academic): reads the "see also" reminders I leave in litnotes as inline footnotes, =[fn::[[id:...][Concept]]]=, and ranks them. Literate doc: =docs/tangles/academic/sp-see-also.org=.

- *Scanner* (=sp-see-also-parse-buffer=): finds inline footnotes with an =id:= link and records the context of each: =quote= or =prose=, and the open question heading above it (keywords come from =sp-org-keywords-research=, so =QUESTION SCOPING READING SYNTHESIZING REVIEW= are open; =STABLE ANSWERED DROPPED= are closed).
- *No buffers visited*: files are read with =insert-file-contents= into a temp buffer in =fundamental-mode= (no hooks, no =org-mode=, no =org-element=). A test checks its footnote ranges against =org-element=. Unsaved open buffers are read from the buffer.
- *Cache*: per file, keyed by mtime and size, kept in =var/cache/see-also-index.eld=; reset when the keywords change. Vulpea gives the litnote list, the titles, and (via =vulpea-db-query-links-to=) the files that link to a concept, so a concept lookup reads only those. The whole-vault index is the fallback.
- *Priority*: two replaceable functions, =sp-see-also-concept-score-function= and =sp-see-also-question-priority-function=. Default: mention weight (prose 1, quote 2, plus the keyword weight when under an open question), times =1 + ln(1 + other litnotes)=.
- *Dashboards*: a =see-also= vulpea-ui widget (concepts by score, then open questions) and a =concept-usage= widget plus =#+BEGIN: concept-usage= dynamic block (litnote, where, under, context).
