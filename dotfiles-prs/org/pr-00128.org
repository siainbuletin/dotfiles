**** Summary

- =vertico-count= is already 13 in =sp-completion=, but the mini-frame was only =vertico-count + 2= frame lines tall. =sp-minibuffer= enlarges the minibuffer text by two steps of =text-scale-mode-step= (about 1.17), so its lines are taller than the frame's and the last candidates were cut off when scrolling.
- =sp-mini-frame--lines= now multiplies the line count by that scale and rounds up.
- Doc and CHANGELOG updated.
