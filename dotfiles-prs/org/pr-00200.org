**** What
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what
:END:
=sp-reading-mode= in =sp-writedesk= (=C-c w r=): a buffer-local minor mode for reading long prose in Org. From Rougier's book-mode and its article example it keeps the margins (8 left, 6 right), the line spacing (1) and the zero fringes; all are =defcustom=s.

- Turns =olivetti-mode= off while active (both use window margins) and back on afterwards if it was on.
- Wraps lines (=visual-line-mode=).
- Records the local state of =line-spacing=, margin and fringe widths, =word-wrap=, =truncate-lines=, =visual-line-mode= and =olivetti-mode= first; toggling off restores each.
- Margins are reapplied to every window on the buffer after window changes.

Only the ideas are used; the code is new (book-mode is GPL-3.0, nothing copied). Left out: the header/mode-line, frame resizing and =message= override, and Rougier's margin bullets and heading markers (listed as not done in the doc).

**** Tested
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: tested
:END:
Emacs 29.3 batch: on sets spacing 1, margin 8, fringe 0, visual-line on, olivetti off; off restores everything (including non-local state) and olivetti on. =tools/tangle-all.el= writes the =.el= identical to the committed one. Not seen on screen in a real frame.
