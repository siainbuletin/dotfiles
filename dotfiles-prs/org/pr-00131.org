**** What
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what
:END:
A global hotkey pops a small floating box; you type =Buy milk #errands !fri= and press Return. A =** TODO= (with =:ID:=, =:CREATED:=, tag and =DEADLINE=) lands under "Tasks" in =inbox.org= and you are back in the app you were in.

- Inline syntax: =#tag= (matched against =org-tag-alist= and the tag registry, =#err= finds =@errands=, TAB completes), =!date= deadline, =^date= scheduled. Dates: =today=, =tom=, =fri=, =+3d=, =2w=, =3nov=, =2026-11-03=.
- Live preview under the prompt shows title, tags and dates, and warns about unknown or ambiguous tags and bad dates.
- Two entry points: =tools/quick-capture= (=emacsclient -e '(sp-quick-capture-popup)'=) and ~org-protocol://sp-quick-capture[?text=…]~ (with =text= it captures silently, for Alfred/Raycast/Shortcuts input fields).
- Module =sp-quick-capture= loads right after =sp-org-capture=; the doc has the macOS setup steps for Raycast, Alfred, Hammerspoon, skhd and Shortcuts.
