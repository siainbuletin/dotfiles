**** Summary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: summary
:END:
- =tools/devonthink-to-litdb.py=: indexes the highlighted pages of DEVONthink PDFs in litdb (one row per highlighted page, source = the record's item address with ~?page=N~, incremental, =--dry-run= first).
- =tools/litdb-query.py=: a search process that keeps litdb's model loaded (words, meaning, or both).
- =sp-litdb= (=C-c k l=, index with =C-c k I=): searches it from Emacs. Hits are =key p. N= candidates of the category =sp-litdb=; Embark actions open the page in DEVONthink, insert a link or =[cite:@key p. N]=, or collect hits with their highlights into a note. Documented as the complement to =org-semantic= (what you wrote) and DEVONthink's own search (every PDF).

Follows #209 (design) and #210 (=ROAM_REFS=).
