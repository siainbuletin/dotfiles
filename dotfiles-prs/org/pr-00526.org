**** What changed
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-changed
:END:
- New module =sp-sourcerefs= (literate doc =docs/tangles/academic/sp-sourcerefs.org=, bullets) and =tests/sp-sourcerefs-test.el=.
- A line =SOURCEREFS text= (optionally after a list bullet) in a litnote is a reference met in the PDF you are reading.
- =M-x sp-sourcerefs-convert= (=C-c o s=), on the region or the buffer:
  - looks for the PDF's reference list: the litnote's citekey (=#+reference:= or =ROAM_REFS=) must equal the citekey of the record in front in DEVONthink; a short =Smith 2017= is widened to the full entry (=sp-cite-open-entry=);
  - matches every line with =sp-cite-match= (BibTeX files, same thresholds); no second matcher;
  - shows a report buffer and asks once;
  - clear matches become =[cite:@key]= (bullet kept), close calls ask you to choose, unmatched lines stay as they are;
  - matched works and unmatched lines go to the =Cited works= table (=sp-cite-match-write-section=): the =TODO unmatched= rows and works without a litnote are what to read next.
- Design doc, README module table and the =require= in =sp-academic= updated. =CHANGELOG.org= not edited.
