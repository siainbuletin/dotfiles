** Description
/Requested by *Sia*/

*Before:* =TAB= stopped on every button: each title, then every TODO of every box.

*After:*

- =TAB= / =S-TAB= walk only the box titles ("General agenda", "Thesis", "Issues", "Deadlines", "Config", "Notes"), wrapping around.
- =<right>= on a title steps into the entries of that box (its TODOs, or the rows of a menu block). =TAB= / =S-TAB= then walk only those entries, wrapping within the box.
- =<left>= goes back to the box's title, where =TAB= walks the titles again.
- =RET= or a click on a title still opens its agenda, on an entry it visits the TODO.
- The key shown at the right of a title, =(a)=, is no longer a separate button (it is still clickable), so a title is one stop. This also fixes titles being two stops since the larger-title change.
- Implementation: buttons carry =sp-dashboard-level= (=title= default, =item=) and =sp-dashboard-box= (the key of their box); =sp-dashboard--scope= holds the box being walked and is reset at each redraw. Doc and CHANGELOG updated.

*Checked* (Emacs 29.3, headless): TAB over titles in order and wrapping, =<right>= into the General agenda entries, TAB and =S-TAB= within them, =<left>= back to the title; layout, privacy/Focus and clicking a TODO unchanged. *Not checked:* a real GUI, and entering a menu block with child rows (same code path, not run).
