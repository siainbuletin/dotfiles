/Requested by *Sia* · [[https://claude.ai/code/project/chan_01KaQfLZaF43QUMN3kcT7pW2?thread=cmsg_01KaQfLZaF43QUMN3kcT7pW2DtCMR6qcoYVuswMQFkPCKF][project thread]]/

Follow-up to #173 for =rel-anno-fa2=.

- *Room between nodes:* graph-fa2 lays nodes out without knowing label sizes, so labels overlapped. Nodes are now drawn =relationship-annotator-fa2-spread= (4) times farther apart on a canvas that many times larger (advice on =graph-fa2-normalise-coords= and its inverse, so drag and click still match). Labels keep their size, so zoom in to read. The engine is now =2d=: in 3d, nodes behind each other overlap whatever the spacing.
- *Box behind each label:* a rounded box in the node's colour at 75% opacity (=relationship-annotator-fa2-box-opacity=); the text takes the theme foreground or background, whichever contrasts. graph-fa2 has no box option, so this post-processes its SVG (advice on =graph-fa2--render-svg=). I chose the box over making the box the node, because the circle still gives the layout and click target.
- *Theme colours:* the node fill is the foreground of a theme face per tag group colour (=relationship-annotator-fa2-group-faces=: orange is =warning=, green is =success=, gray is =shadow=, ...), and text, edge labels and edges follow =default= and =shadow=. It reads the theme each time the command runs.

Tested in headless Emacs 29 with the real graph-fa2: the SVG gets the boxes, and across 12 to 60 boxes (2d), overlapping pairs went from 6 to 33 at spread 1 to none at spread 4. In 3d some still overlapped, hence the 2d engine. Not seen on screen: how the colours look on your themes and the zoom level. Literate doc and CHANGELOG updated.
