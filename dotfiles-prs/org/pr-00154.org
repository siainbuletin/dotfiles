**** What
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what
:END:
After #152, =M-x= was fine at init but glitched again once Org was in use. The dimming remapped every face with a foreground (over a thousand, from =face-list=) in each buffer behind the posframe. Every redisplay of that buffer looks faces up in that remap list, so a busy Org buffer (indent, numbering, tables, tag groups) redraws slowly on each keystroke in the posframe.

=sp-posframe-dim= now remaps =default= and the faces found on the visible text only (=sp-posframe--visible-faces=, a walk of the =face= property from =window-start= to =window-end=). The look is the same; text scrolled into view while the minibuffer is open is not dimmed.

The doc has two extra checks for the mirrored-buffer symptom: whether =vertico-buffer-mode= is on at the same time as the posframe, and =(setq sp-posframe-dim-always nil)= to rule the dimming out.
