- *Request:* the key lists in the header lines of the linting and batch interfaces do not wrap; show them in the echo area like =nano-help.el= of nano-emacs.
- *New module =sp-echo-hints=* (doc =docs/tangles/funct/sp-echo-hints.org=):
  - =sp-echo-hints-mode= shows a buffer's hints when Emacs is idle and the echo area is empty, so a command's own message is never replaced.
  - A hint is =(KEY . LABEL)=; the key is bold. Hints that do not fit the frame width are dropped from the end and marked with "...".
  - Not written to =*Messages*=.
  - Options: =sp-echo-hints-delay=, =sp-echo-hints-separator=, face =sp-echo-hints-key=.
