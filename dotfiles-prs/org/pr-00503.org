**** What changed
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-changed
:END:
Two separate commands, in this order:

1. =sp-bibtex-lint-fix-date= (=C-; L b f d=): the automatic one, ISO dates only.
   - ={2021}= → ~year = {2021}~
   - ={2021-05}= → =year=, ~month = {5}~
   - ={2021-05-14}= → =year=, ~month = {5}~; the day is dropped (and an existing =day= field goes with it)
   - the new fields take the place of =date=; braces or quotes are kept
2. =sp-bibtex-lint-fix-date-text= (=C-; L b f D=): run it after the first, for the dates that one leaves.
   - month as a word: =May 14, 2021=, =14th May 2021=, =May. 2021=, =2021 Mai=; month names in English, German, French and Spanish (=sp-bibtex-date-month-names=)
   - numbers: =14.5.2021= and =14-5-2021= (day first), =5/14/2021=, =2021/5/14=, =2021-5-4=
   - =3/4/2021= (both numbers could be the month) is reported and left, unless =sp-bibtex-date-numeric-order= is =day-first= or =month-first=; a number over 12 settles it
   - it also reads ISO dates, so on its own it does everything; after the first it has only the rest

Both:

- =@misc= entries (=sp-bibtex-date-keep-types=) are not touched: their =date= stays whole, with the day.
- Never overwritten silently (entry left as it is, listed under "... left as they are", with the reason):
  - an existing =year= that differs from the date; an existing =month= that is another month (=5=, =05=, ={May}=, =may= count as the same)
  - ranges, seasons (=Spring 2021=), approximate dates (=c. 1850=, =2021?=), impossible dates, a =date= that is a macro
- A field that already holds the same value stays; only =date= is dropped. An empty field is replaced.
- Like the other fixes: the command opens the report, nothing is written until =A=; =A= asks once, copies the file to =sp-bibtex-lint-backup-directory= and writes; =d=/=u= drop and bring back entries (=C-; L b r=).
- The report of =sp-bibtex-lint-dryrun= has a section and a button for the ISO fix; =sp-bibtex-lint-convert= has an entry for each.
- Options: =sp-bibtex-date-keep-types=, =sp-bibtex-date-month-format= (=%d= gives =5=, =%02d= gives =05=), =sp-bibtex-date-numeric-order=, =sp-bibtex-date-month-names=.
- Docs: section "Date fields to year and month" in =sp-academic.org= (bullets); =L b f d= and =L b f D= in =sp-keys.org=. No shims, =CHANGELOG.org= not edited.
