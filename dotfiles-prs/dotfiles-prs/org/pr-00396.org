**** What
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what
:END:
- =sp-see-also-show= and =sp-see-also-show-concept= now draw a decorated view (=sp-see-also-view-mode=), laid out like the DG context pane of =discourse-graphs=.
- A group per concept, open question or litnote: a coloured heading (=outline-1=) with score and counts as small print.
- An entry per mention: heading line (marks, place, question), then the context as an indented quote.
- Each entry has a background from =sp-highlight= (the Modus =bg-hl-line=), so it follows the active theme.
- Headings and contexts are clickable (mouse-1 or =RET=) and visit the footnote; a concept heading opens its concept view.
- Keys: =n=/=p= headings, =TAB= fold one entry, =S-TAB= fold all, =g= redraw, =q= close.
