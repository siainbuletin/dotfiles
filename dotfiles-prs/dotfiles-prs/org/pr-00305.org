**** Why
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: why

After pointing Hammerspoon at =~/.config/hammerspoon=, it failed with =init.lua:3: attempt to index a boolean value (global 'stackline')=. =require "stackline"= finds the empty top-level =stackline.lua= (it returns =true=), so =stackline:init()= errors, and the AeroSpace keys on the last line never load. That is why Caps Lock and Option did nothing for AeroSpace.

**** What
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what
:END:
- =init.lua= requires =aerospace-keys= first.
- stackline is loaded inside =pcall=; a failure is printed to the Console (=stackline not loaded: ...=) and no longer stops anything.
