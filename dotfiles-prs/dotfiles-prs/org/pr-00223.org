Follows #212. In =sp-author-notes=:

*=and= separators*

- =Ann Lee and Bo Roe=, =Smith, John and Doe, Jane= and =Smith, John and Ann Lee= still split on =and= as before.
- A list with commas that ends in =and= (=Ann Lee, Bo Roe, and Cy Poe=) used to split at the =and= and leave =Ann Lee, Bo Roe,= as one flipped name. It is now one disambiguation case (as in #212) with all the names proposed.

*Dropping candidates*

- In the report, =d= drops the candidate on the line from the plan and refreshes the preview, =u= restores it, =A= writes what is left, =g= refreshes keeping the drops. A note (litnote, author note, note to tag) is dropped as a file (not read or changed); an author to create is dropped by name (no note, the name stays plain). Dropped items are listed at the end of the report.
- =M-x sp-author-notes= now asks =y= (write all), =r= (stay in the report to review) or =q=. =M-x sp-author-notes-preview= forgets earlier drops.
- I chose dropping over marking: the default is "write everything shown", and =d= removes the exceptions.
