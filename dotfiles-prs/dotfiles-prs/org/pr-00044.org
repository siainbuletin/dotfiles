Follow-up to #31, whose splash looked unchanged on Sia's machine.

Before: =M-x sp-splash-screen= only switched to an existing =*splash*= buffer, so the new art never appeared until that buffer was killed. When the art was drawn, every other character was dropped, because =sp-splash--put-art-char= skipped a character when the line already ended at its column.

After: called interactively, =sp-splash-screen= kills an old =*splash*= first and redraws. The art is complete, and the dark/light greys are a little stronger (=#454b5c= / =#cfd3dc=). =sp-splash.org= is updated in step, with a CHANGELOG entry.

How it was checked: in headless Emacs 29.3 (stubbing =nerd-icons-faicon=, 120x50 frame) the splash shows the full lambda behind Agenda/Config/Mail/Notes/Projects with the text on top, and =sp-splash-art-right= moves it and redraws. The doc's blocks tangle to the same text as the =.el=.

Not checked: colours and layout in a real GUI frame with the PNG-capable build, and your themes.
