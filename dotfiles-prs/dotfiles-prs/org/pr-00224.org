**** Summary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: summary
:END:
Follows #221 (merged). Changes what =sp-litnotes-titles= writes:

- *Title:* last names only, from =pub-author=, =pub-year= and =pub-title=: =Reuten (2014) The value form as a social form=. Two authors =A and B=, three =A, B and C=, four or more the first two and =et al.= (=A, B et al.=; =sp-litnotes-titles-et-al-from=). =pub-author= is read as =Last, First=, =First Last= (lower-case particles stay with the surname), =[[id:..][Name]]= links and quoted names, split at =;=, =and= or between links.
- *Failures:* a missing =pub-year= fails (no =n.d.=), as do a missing or empty =pub-title=, a missing =pub-author= and an ambiguous one (a piece with several commas). The report lists them under =Failed: ...= with what the note has, and counts them.
- *Asking:* =sp-litnotes-titles-apply= offers to fill in the failed items: one question per missing value (title, authors as =Last, First; Last, First=, year), an empty answer skips. The answers are used for the title only; the note's meta lines are not changed. The report is shown again, then the single write confirmation.
- *File name:* the first citation key alone (=ROAM_REFS=, or =#+reference:= for notes #210's conversion has not reached), =reuten2014value.org=. Not renamed and listed: no key, a key whose file exists, a key two notes share, case-only differences.
- Still: report first, time-stamped backups, =:ID:= checked, Vulpea db updated, =file:= links to old names listed.
