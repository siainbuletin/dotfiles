Stacked on #48, which is stacked on #45. Merge in that order; GitHub retargets each PR as its base merges.

Before: the splash colours were two fixed greys chosen for dark or light backgrounds, plus the box face.

After: a small framework of colour roles.

- A role (=art=, =banner=, =footer=, =accent-1..3=) takes its colour from a face of the current theme (=shadow=, =font-lock-keyword-face=, ...) mixed towards the default background, so the lambda stays quiet and the accents stay vivid in any theme. =sp-splash-palette-roles= says which theme face, attribute and mix each role uses, so adding a role is one line.
- =sp-splash-palettes= lets you choose colours for a particular theme by hand; roles not listed are still derived.
- The menu boxes are coloured with the accent faces in turn (=sp-splash-accent-faces=), so each block has its own colour.
- The splash is recoloured and redrawn when a theme is enabled or disabled (=enable-theme-functions=, =disable-theme-functions=; switch off with =sp-splash-palette-follow-theme=). =sp-splash-apply-palette= and =sp-splash-show-palette= do it and show it by hand.
- =sp-splash.org= has a new "Colour palette" section and tangles to the same text as the =.el=; CHANGELOG entry added.

Checked in headless Emacs 29.3 with faces set by hand: the roles came out as derived colours, a per-theme override won, the three boxes got the three accent faces, and enabling a test theme with a red =shadow= recoloured the art. Not checked: how the derived colours look on your real themes; tune the mix values in =sp-splash-palette-roles= if the art is too faint or too strong.
