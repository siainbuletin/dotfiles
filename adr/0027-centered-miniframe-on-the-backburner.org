#+title: 0027 Centered minibuffer (posframe) is on the backburner and off by default
#+date: 2026-11-14
#+status: accepted

* Context
- =sp-posframe= shows the minibuffer (prompt and Vertico candidates) in a centred child frame.
- It replaced =sp-mini-frame= after =M-x= hung Emacs on macOS; the cause of the hang was never found.
- Since then the module has needed many fixes: glitching text in =M-x=, a stray =*Minibuf-1*= window, text size, width (#136 to #163).
- I do not like how the centred minibuffer works at the moment, and I do not want to spend more time on it now.

* Decision
- =sp-posframe-enabled= defaults to =nil=: a fresh start has no posframe and the minibuffer is at the bottom of the frame.
- =M-x sp-posframe-toggle= is unchanged and still turns the posframe on for a session.
- The centred miniframe setup is marked as on the backburner, not removed.
  - The =sp-posframe.el= commentary and the =sp-posframe-enabled= docstring say so.
  - The literate doc =docs/tangles/ui/sp-posframe.org= starts with an archived status banner.
  - The module table in =README.org= marks the entry as archived.
- No new work goes into the module until I take it up again; its rebuild plan stays in the doc.

* Alternatives considered
- *Delete the module*: loses the notes and the rebuild plan, and the toggle is cheap to keep.
- *Leave it on by default*: I keep hitting the problems that made me dislike it.
- *Go back to =mini-frame=*: it had the same hang and never traced to a cause; see the doc.

* Consequences
- The default minibuffer is the normal one, so the glitches of the posframe do not show.
- =sp-posframe-width-fraction= and the border padding do nothing while it is off.
- =sp-citations= falls back to the frame width when the posframe is off (it already checks =vertico-posframe-mode=).
- Anyone who set =sp-posframe-enabled= in their config keeps their choice; only the default changed.
- To resume: set =sp-posframe-enabled= to =t=, follow "Rebuilding the setup from zero" in the doc, and supersede this record.
- /Untested/ on my Mac: the change was made from a Linux container.
