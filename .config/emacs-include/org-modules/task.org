* Global task management

Since I work a lot in writing projects that have to deal with a lot of open-ended questions, I find myself needing to just add a task to a paragraph, to signify that the information there is incomplete and needs to be greatly improved.

One may argue that such tasks would be useless because you'd be cluttering up your agenda, but when your goal is to single in on particular subtrees (in this case defined as files, and not in the sense that org-mode uses the terminology), you can go there and see _especially_ all of the dependencies between your tasks (see [[#Dependencies between tasks]]. Everything can be formulated as a task after all, and fragmenting your workflow into such actionable chunks helps for the thinking process and being able to procrastinate a lot less.
