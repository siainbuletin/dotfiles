Sets up Embark, which offers a menu of actions for whatever is at point or under the minibuffer cursor (a file, a buffer, a symbol, a URL and so on), together with its Consult integration. It is loaded from =init.el= among the utility modules.

* Embark

** embark

Binds =C-.= to =embark-act= (choose an action for the target), =M-.= to =embark-dwim= (run the default action) and =C-h B= to =embark-bindings=. Note that =M-.= normally runs =xref-find-definitions=, so this binding takes that over globally and =embark-dwim= becomes the way to jump to definitions. The =:init= block sets =prefix-help-command= so that pressing =C-h= after a prefix key shows Embark's searchable list of the bindings under that prefix.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp -i
(use-package embark
  :ensure t
  :bind
  (("C-." . embark-act)
   ("M-." . embark-dwim)
   ("C-h B" . embark-bindings))

  :init
  (setq prefix-help-command #'embark-prefix-help-command))
#+end_src

** embark-consult

Loads the glue between Embark and Consult once both are present, which adds actions such as exporting Consult results to a collect or occur buffer.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp -i
(use-package embark-consult
  :ensure t
  :after (embark consult))
#+end_src
